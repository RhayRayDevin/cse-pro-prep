HOW TO INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE
----------------------------
1. Transfer the entire "cse-pro-prep" folder to your phone
   (or upload index.html + other files to any free static host like GitHub Pages, Netlify, or open locally).

2. Open index.html in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iOS).

3. Tap the browser menu:
   - Android Chrome: ⋮ → "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
   - iOS Safari: Share button → "Add to Home Screen"

4. Launch the icon from your home screen. It now works offline.
